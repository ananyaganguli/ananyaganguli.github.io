My story, in chapters

My career began in chemical engineering, then moved into code, and somewhere along the way realised the thing I liked most was explaining how things work.

Chapter one · 2007 to 2014

The engineer

NIT Durgapur, then Mindtree in Bangalore

I studied chemical engineering and then spent three years building user interfaces in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I still use that grounding every week. When a product team explains their API, I can follow along, and I can build the landing page myself if I need to.

Chapter two · 2014 to 2017

Learning the language of buyers

MICA in Ahmedabad, then Sulekha.com in Chennai

I went back to school for management communication and came out running SEM, SEO and affiliate campaigns side by side. At Sulekha I brought cost per acquisition down 40%, and the work was shortlisted for a Think with Google case study.

Chapter three · 2017 to 2019

Thinking globally at LinkedIn

LinkedIn, Gurgaon

I planned demand gen, vertical and account-based programs with teams in North America, EMEA and APAC. I cross-sold Sales Navigator to existing customers, grew its customer base 30% and took usage to 86%. I also got LinkedIn India onto page one for 20 target keywords, and our team was named Top Performing Team, APAC.

Chapter four · 2019 to 2020

Coming home to Kolkata

Tata Metaliks, corporate communications

Back in Bengal, I worked with the agencies behind a Tata Group industrial brand. Organic social reach rose 56%, and the company recorded its first ever NPS of 8.1.

Chapter five · 2020 to 2022

A new country and a blank page

CadMakers, Vancouver

I moved to Canada and joined a construction tech startup where I owned the whole marketing budget and reported to the CEO. I built go-to-market for two SaaS products from scratch and learned construction from the people who do it, through customer interviews and long sessions with the product team.

Chapter six · 2022 to 2026

Making payments make sense

Shift4, formerly Worldline North America

I ran the content calendar, wrote for technical buyers, ghostwrote for executives and owned paid campaigns across Google, LinkedIn and Meta. Sales asked for a fresh set of collateral with every feature release. Over my time there, qualified leads grew 15% year over year and closed leads grew 27%.

Chapter seven · 2026 to now

Building a brand from the first word

Marketing consultant, early-stage AI startup

I'm helping an early-stage AI startup find its voice. The product is a shared thinking workspace for teams, and there's no budget for paid growth, so every word has to work hard. I'm building the go-to-market and content strategy, shaping the brand voice, and writing the website and blog articles, with an eye on showing up in AI answer engines as well as Google search.

Alongside this, I'm looking for my next full-time team, somewhere storytelling and performance marketing sit at the same table.

How I work

Whether it's a case study, a launch or a paid campaign, I follow the same three steps.

01

Listen first

I talk to the product team, the customer and the people in sales who hear objections every day. The best material is usually something someone mentioned in passing.

02

Find the tension

Every good story has a before and after. I look for the moment a buyer would recognise as their own problem, and build the piece around it.

03

Ship, measure, repeat

I A/B test headlines, landing pages and emails, and track results in GA4, HubSpot, Marketo and Salesforce. Then I put more effort into whatever's working.
